# INAF-OACT-AI 🚀

### Artificial Intelligence & Scientific Computing at INAF – Catania Astrophysical Observatory

Welcome to the GitHub organization for **Artificial Intelligence (AI) research and development activities** at the **INAF – Catania Astrophysical Observatory (INAF-OACT)**, Italy.

## 🔭 About Us

We develop and apply **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and advanced scientific computing techniques** to address challenges in astrophysics, astronomy, and related scientific and technological domains.

Our activities include:

- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning** – AI models for scientific data analysis.
- **Astronomical Data Analysis** – Automated processing, classification, and characterization of astronomical data.
- **Computer Vision** – Image processing, source detection, segmentation, and pattern recognition.
- **Scientific Computing** – High-performance and distributed computing, data processing, and scalable workflows.
- **AI Tools & Applications** – Reusable software tools, libraries, and AI-based services for research.

## 💻 Our Repositories

This organization hosts research software, AI/ML models, scientific pipelines, experimental tools, and other resources developed within our research activities.

We aim to promote **open science, reproducibility, and software reuse**, encouraging collaboration across the astronomical and broader scientific communities.

## 🤝 Collaboration & Contributions

We welcome scientific collaborations, feedback, and contributions from researchers, developers, and students interested in AI applications to scientific research.

Please refer to individual repositories for documentation, licensing information, and contribution guidelines.
